Splander Jobs

Your job search co-pilot

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-11

Splander Jobs is a Chrome extension that helps you track job applications you find on LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, AllJobs, JobNet, JobFox and Jobify, and fill in application forms. This page describes exactly what data the extension handles, where it lives, and what we do (and don't do) with it.

Summary, in one paragraph

Splander Jobs runs entirely in your browser. Every job you track is saved to chrome.storage.local on your own device. The extension does not have a server-side database of your applications, and we never see or transmit your job-search data. The only personal information that ever leaves your device is your email address, and only if you choose to submit it to the Premium waitlist on the Premium tab or to be notified about our new jobs website. Everything else that can leave your device does so only after you turn it on: signing in to sync across devices (section 3), the AI features (section 8) and CV open tracking (section 9). The extension also sends an anonymous, opt-out usage heartbeat (a random id + coarse counts, no personal data) so we can count active users — see section 5 — and, only when you explicitly submit the in-app feedback form or tap a rating, your message plus those same coarse, non-personal details — see section 6. Read on for the details.

What data we handle

1. Job-application data (job sites)

When you use Splander Jobs on a job page on LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, AllJobs, JobNet, JobFox or Jobify, the extension reads the page's contents — title, company, location, posting URL, salary, and a few similar fields — so it can attach a status pill to the listing and let you save it to your tracker.

  • Where it lives: chrome.storage.local, a sandboxed per-extension area of your own browser profile.
  • Where it does NOT live: Nowhere else, unless you sign in and keep cloud sync on (section 3). We never send it to a third party.
  • How long: Until you uninstall the extension or clear your browser profile.
  • How to export or delete: Settings → Your data → Export JSON backup (download a copy). Uninstalling Splander Jobs or clearing extension storage deletes everything.

2. Premium waitlist email (only if you submit it)

The Premium tab inside the side panel includes a "Coming soon" preview and an email waitlist form. If, and only if, you type in your email and click "Join the waitlist", Splander Jobs sends that one email address to our Supabase project (hcwnozzjsgqibhpebkhx.supabase.co) so we can notify you when Premium opens for beta access.

  • What is sent: Your email address, the Splander Jobs version that submitted it, and your browser's User-Agent string. Nothing else.
  • Where it lives: A waitlist_signups row in our Supabase database plus an unconfirmed Supabase Auth user record with the same email.
  • What we use it for: Notifying you when Premium opens for beta and (if we promote you to a beta tester) signing you in for Premium cloud sync.
  • What we do NOT use it for: Marketing emails unrelated to Splander Jobs, selling to third parties, advertising, or recovery of your local tracking data (we do not store your tracking data on our servers).
  • How to delete: Email dev@splander.com from the address you signed up with and we'll remove your waitlist row and any associated auth user.

3. Splander Jobs account and cloud sync (only if you sign in)

Signing in is optional. You can log in with Google or with an email and password from the onboarding screen or the Premium tab. When you sign in, cloud sync is turned on by default so your tracker follows you across devices. You can turn it off any time in Settings, and your choice is remembered.

  • What syncs while cloud sync is on: the jobs you track (with their status history and notes), your ignore list, job leads you saved from job pages, your contacts and outreach log, your CVs (text and the files you uploaded), and your settings, including the profile and saved answers autofill uses.
  • What never syncs: anything from Gmail (section 4) and the API key you enter for your own AI provider (section 8). Both stay on your device.
  • Where it lives: our Supabase project, where every row belongs to your account and no other user can read it.
  • Sign-out: Settings → Sign out clears the session on this device.
  • Account deletion: Email dev@splander.com and we'll delete the auth user and all rows associated with it.

4. Optional Gmail integration (you grant per-account)

Settings → Gmail lets you connect a Gmail account so Splander Jobs can detect recruiter replies and auto-advance your application status. This uses Google's read-only Gmail scope (gmail.readonly).

  • Where your email is processed: entirely in your browser. Emails are read and matched to your applications on your device.
  • What stays on your device: all email content — subjects, senders, message text, message ids, the list of emails already read, and job leads found in job-alert emails. It is stored in chrome.storage.local and is never uploaded to Splander's servers, and never sent to an AI model.
  • What can leave your device: only if you turn on cloud sync, the result syncs — an application's company, job title and status. No email content is part of it.
  • Disconnect: any time, from Settings → Gmail.

The Chrome Web Store build of Splander Jobs is read-only with respect to your Gmail — it never writes anything to your mailbox. (A separate full build, which is not distributed on the Chrome Web Store, additionally offers an optional "status labels" feature that, after a second explicit opt-in, adds its own status labels to the emails it already classifies and can archive already-filed mail out of the Inbox; even then it only changes label membership and never sends, deletes, or edits email content.)

5. Anonymous usage analytics (on by default, you can opt out)

To understand how many people use Splander Jobs and which broad features get used, the extension sends a once-a-day anonymous "heartbeat" to our Supabase project. Turn it off any time in Settings → About → "Share anonymous usage data"; when off, the extension sends no telemetry at all.

  • What is sent: A random install identifier (generated on first run — not derived from you or your device), the app version, your browser UI language, your operating system, your plan tier (free/beta/premium), and three coarse flags: whether cloud sync is on, whether Gmail is connected, and a bucketed range for how many jobs you've saved (e.g. "10–49"). If you are signed in, the heartbeat is associated with your account so we can measure sign-up conversion.
  • What is NOT sent: No email address, no job / company / CV / message content, no URLs, no page contents — ever. The server additionally discards anything outside the short list above.
  • Where it lives: A telemetry_events row in our Supabase project. It is never sold or shared with a third party, and no third-party analytics SDK is embedded.

6. Feedback you submit (only when you send it)

The Settings → Feedback & Support page — and the occasional in-app "was this helpful?" prompt — let you send a feature idea, a bug report, or a rating. A feedback message reaches our Supabase project only when you click Send or tap a rating; nothing is sent just for opening the page.

  • What is sent: Your message text (only what you type), the random install identifier from section 5, and the same coarse, non-personal details the heartbeat sends — app version, operating system, UI language, and a coarse label for the job site you're on (e.g. "linkedin"). A bug report shows you exactly these details before you send. If you are signed in, it is associated with your account.
  • What is NOT sent: No job / company / CV / email content, no URLs, no page contents, and no diagnostic logs — ever. The server additionally discards anything outside the short list above.
  • The one exception is the "new jobs website" card: if you choose to enter your email there to be notified, that email is stored (tagged separately from the Premium waitlist). You never have to enter it to use the rest of feedback.
  • Where it lives: A feedback row in our Supabase project. It is never sold or shared with a third party.
  • How to delete: Email dev@splander.com.

7. Application-form autofill (you turn it on)

When you turn on autofill and open an application form, on LinkedIn Easy Apply or a supported applicant-tracking system such as Greenhouse, Lever, Workable, Comeet or Workday, Splander Jobs reads the form's fields so it can fill them.

  • What it fills in: your own details from Settings → Profile & links, your saved answers, and the CV you chose, all stored on your device (and synced only as described in section 3).
  • Where that data goes: only into the form on the employer's page, exactly as if you typed it. It reaches the employer only when the form is submitted.
  • Who submits: by default, you. Splander Jobs fills the form and waits for you to review it and press the form's own Submit. If you choose Auto mode in Settings, it presses Submit for you, but only when every required question is answered. It never invents an answer to a question you haven't answered before, and it never ticks a consent box for you.

8. AI features (only if you turn them on)

The free CV match runs entirely on your device. The AI features (AI CV match, resume tailoring, profile score and drafted messages) send the CV and job text they need to an AI provider:

  • With your own API key: your browser sends the request directly to the provider you choose (Google Gemini, OpenAI or Anthropic), under that provider's terms. The key is stored only on your device and never synced.
  • With Premium: the request goes through our server to Google Gemini. We do not store the CV or job text.
  • Never sent: email content from Gmail.

9. CV open tracking (only if you turn it on, signed in)

CV open tracking tells you whether someone opened the CV you sent. When it is on, the copy of your CV you attach is marked: a Word file gets a tiny image that loads from our server when the file is opened, and the links in a PDF go through our server first.

  • What we record on an open or click: the time, whether it looks automated (an applicant-tracking system or link preview), and a shortened one-way hash of the viewer's IP address and browser. We never store the IP address itself, and the hash can't be used to follow a viewer across links.
  • Who sees it: only you, on the job it belongs to.
  • Turn off: Settings → Profile & links → CV open tracking.

What we do NOT do

  • No third-party analytics SDKs. Splander Jobs does not embed Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Segment, or any similar third-party tracker. The only usage data we collect is the first-party anonymous heartbeat described in section 5 above, which you can turn off in Settings → About.
  • No advertising. We do not sell access to your data to advertisers or recruiters.
  • No third-party trackers. We do not embed third-party scripts.
  • No sending on your behalf. Splander Jobs never sends emails or messages for you, and it submits an application form only if you turned on Auto mode (section 7). The Chrome Web Store build is a strictly read-only Gmail client.

Cookies

Splander Jobs does not set cookies on any website. It uses chrome.storage.local (not cookies) for its own state.

Permissions, and why we ask for them

  • storage / unlimitedStorage: Save your tracked applications locally.
  • sidePanel: Open the Splander Jobs side panel.
  • alarms: Run the daily streak refresh and the Gmail poll on a timer.
  • identity: Used by the Gmail OAuth flow (you opt in) and the Premium Google sign-in flow (you opt in).
  • Host permissions for the job sites (linkedin.com, indeed.com, glassdoor.com, alljobs.co.il, jobnet.co.il, jobs.scalefox.ai, jobify360.co.il): Display status badges on job listings and save the jobs you choose.
  • Host permissions for applicant-tracking systems (such as greenhouse.io, lever.co, workable.com, comeet.co, myworkdayjobs.com): Fill in application forms when you use autofill (section 7).

We request no host permission for our own server or for Google's APIs: the waitlist, sign-in, sync and the Gmail integration use ordinary web requests that need no extra permission.

(The "Export JSON backup" feature in Settings saves a file using your browser's normal download, so it needs no extra permission. Likewise, opening our jobs website, the Chrome Web Store review page, or the post-uninstall survey just opens a normal browser tab and needs no extra permission.)

Children's privacy

Splander Jobs is intended for job seekers, who are typically adults. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13.
